Combo backtest

Every indicator condition (compared to its own trailing rolling median) exhaustively crossed with every other indicator condition and price-action signal - ALL must agree on direction on the same candle - and backtested for real PnL the same realistic way as a single signal (entry at next open, stop/target bracket, risk-based sizing, fees). Most combinations naturally get zero/few fires since requiring several conditions to align exactly is rare; only profitable combinations are shown below, ranked by total PnL so the biggest profit/capital-growth combination is always row 1.

Indicator contribution

How often each base indicator/signal appears across every profitable combo, and its "lift" - how much a combo's total PnL differs from the overall average when that indicator is present. High appearance + positive lift = a real driver. High appearance + negative (or ~0) lift = along for the ride, not actually helping.

Possibly redundant indicator pairs

Numeric indicator pairs correlated at |r| >= 0.9 (e.g. RSI_7 vs RSI_14) - the search space carries less independent signal than the raw column count suggests.

Perfect-predictor scan (100% win rate)

Every combo that hit a literal 100% win rate, ranked by a Wilson-score confidence lower bound (not the raw 100%) so a 9-trade fluke doesn't read the same as an 80-trade result. Reliability also factors in combo size - deeper combos (more ANDed conditions) are inherently more overfit-prone.

Direction-right-but-target-missed diagnostic

Our backtest uses a FIXED take-profit/stop-loss bracket. A condition can correctly predict direction (directional_accuracy%) far more often than it actually hits that fixed target (target_hit_rate%) - the gap is exactly "direction was right, fixed target missed it." suggested_tp%/suggested_sl% are the condition's own median favorable/adverse move size - a data-driven bracket instead of the fixed one. Sort by gap% to see where the fixed bracket is failing worst.
